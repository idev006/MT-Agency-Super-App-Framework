2. Verify current consolidated law and all applicable amended legislation and PDPC notifications; not yet completed.
3. Identify lawful basis and issue privacy notices for staff and visitors.
4. Limit fields to plate/province, vehicle description, responsible contact, temporary pass and essential timestamps.
5. QR must be opaque; login and server authorization before showing phone numbers.
6. Establish retention, data subject request handling, breach procedures and access logs.
7. Review Google Sheets, Apps Script and Drive data access, credentials and cloud transfer implications.
8. Obtain formal organizational legal/DPO sign-off before operational rollout.
